How to play

  • Build each foundation (top right) up by suit, from Ace to King. Get all 52 cards there to win.
  • In the seven columns, stack cards down in alternating colours: a red 9 on a black 10. You can move a whole run at once.
  • Only a King (or a run starting with one) can go in an empty column.
  • Tap the stock (top left) to turn cards over. When it runs out, tap again to go through the pile again.
  • Drag cards, or tap one to send it to the best place. Undo takes back a move.
  • Score: 10 for each card onto a foundation, 5 for each card from the pile to a column, 5 for each card turned face up. Taking a card back off a foundation costs 15, going through the pile again costs 100 (20 when turning 3), and you lose 2 points every 10 seconds. Winning adds a time bonus: the faster, the bigger.
