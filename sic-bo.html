How to play

  • Bet on how three dice will land, then roll. Every winning bet is paid on the same roll.
  • Small (total 4 to 10) and Big (11 to 17) pay 1 to 1, but lose to any triple.
  • Totals pay by how rare they are: 4 or 17 pays 60 to 1, down to 6 to 1 for 9 to 12.
  • Doubles (two of a number) pay 10 to 1, a specific triple 180 to 1, any triple 30 to 1.
  • Combinations pay 5 to 1 if both numbers show.
  • Single numbers pay 1 to 1 for each die showing it (2 to 1 on two dice, 3 to 1 on three).
  • These are the standard Macau pays.
