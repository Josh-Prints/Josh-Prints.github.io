How to play

  • You and the dealer each get one card. Higher card wins and pays 1 to 1. Aces are high; suits don't matter.
  • On a tie you choose: Surrender and get half your bet back, or Go to war by matching your bet.
  • At war, three cards are burned and you each get one more. If yours is higher, the war bet wins 1 to 1 and your first bet comes back. Tie again and both win. If the dealer's is higher, you lose both.
  • The Tie side bet pays 10 to 1 if the first two cards tie.
  • Six decks, shuffled every hand.
