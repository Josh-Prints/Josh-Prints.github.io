How to play

  • Bet on Player, Banker or Tie, then deal. The hand closest to 9 wins.
  • Cards count face value; 10s and picture cards count 0, aces 1. Only the last digit of the total counts (7 + 8 = 5).
  • Nobody chooses to draw: the standard third-card rules decide. Either side on 8 or 9 is a natural and both stand.
  • Banker wins pay 0.95 to 1 (5% commission). A tie pays 8 to 1 and Player and Banker bets push.
  • Pair bets win if that side's first two cards are a pair, and pay 11 to 1.
  • Eight decks, reshuffled when the cut card comes out. The board below the table shows this shoe's results.
