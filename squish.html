Squish

  • Bubbles at the top float until you shoot them.
  • Drag away from a bubble to aim it any way, even up. The further you pull, the faster it flies. Right click, or let go close to the bubble, to cancel.
  • Matching numbers merge and double whenever they touch, floating or not. If one was still floating, the new bubble floats too.
  • The pile presses on the two discs in the side walls. If both get squashed flat for a few seconds, or the pile reaches the floating strip, it's game over.
