Squish

  • Bubbles at the top float until you shoot them.
  • Pull a bubble back like a slingshot and let go. It flies the opposite way, even up. The further you pull, the faster it goes. Right click, or let go close to the bubble, to cancel.
  • Matching numbers merge and double when one of them has been shot into the other. If one was still floating, the new bubble floats too.
  • The pile presses on the two discs in the side walls. If both get squashed flat for a few seconds, it's game over.
