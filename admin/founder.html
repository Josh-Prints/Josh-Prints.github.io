Close the shop when you can't take work on. The order form stops accepting anything and shows your message instead. Orders already placed carry on as normal, and you can still add one by hand from the admin side.

🛒 Accepting new orders

Customers earn store credit automatically when a signed-in customer's order is marked "Sold" — a percentage of the quoted price, added to their balance. They can use it toward future orders (you apply it per-order on the order card).

💳 Store credit enabled

Codes customers can enter when placing an order. Entering one doesn't change any price automatically — like store credit and the signup discount, you see it on the order card and factor it into the quote you type in.

Two-way chat. With a bot added, every order gets its own Discord thread — customer messages appear there and anything you type back lands in their order chat. Create a bot at discord.com/developers → New Application → Bot → Reset Token, and turn on Message Content Intent on that same Bot page or your replies will arrive blank. Invite it to your server, and if the channel is private add the bot under Edit Channel → Permissions → Add members or roles.
