About these terms

Voxprints is a small 3D printing business in Australia, founded by Joshua Paterson and co-owned with James Webley ("we", "us"). These terms cover using voxprints.com and ordering prints from us. By using the site or placing an order you agree to them. If you don't agree, please don't use the site.

Questions? Email josh@voxprints.com or message us from your order page.

Accounts

You can order without an account. If you create one (with Google or an email code), please use your real name, keep access to your email secure, and tell us if you think someone else has used your account. You're responsible for orders placed through your account.

Orders and quotes

  • Requests aren't orders yet. Sending a request, uploading a file, or using the cost estimator doesn't create a contract. The estimator is a rough guide only.
  • Quotes. We review every request and send a quote. Your order is confirmed once you accept the quote and we confirm we'll make it.
  • We can say no. We may decline or cancel any request, for example if it can't be printed well, would infringe someone else's rights, is a weapon or part of one, is illegal or unsafe, or is offensive. If we cancel after you've paid, we'll refund you in full.
  • Your order link. Each order has a private tracking link. Anyone with the link can see the order and its messages, so keep it to yourself.

Your files and designs

  • You keep ownership of files, models and ideas you send us.
  • You confirm you have the right to have them printed, and that printing them won't infringe anyone's copyright, trade marks or other rights.
  • You let us use them only to quote, design, print and support your order. We won't sell them or give them to anyone else, except the service providers listed in our privacy policy that store them for us.
  • If we design a model for you, it's made for your order. We won't sell copies of it to others without asking you.
  • We'll only show photos of your print publicly (for example in our designs gallery) if you've said that's okay.

Prices and payment

  • Prices are in Australian dollars. The price that applies is the one in the quote you accept.
  • For now, payment is in cash when you collect your order, unless we agree something else with you.
  • If the job changes after you accept the quote (for example you ask for a different size or material), we'll send an updated quote before going ahead.

Store credit and promo codes

  • Store credit is a thank-you we may add to your account after completed orders. It can only be used towards future Voxprints orders. It has no cash value and can't be exchanged for money or transferred to someone else.
  • Promo codes and sign-up discounts apply as described when they're offered. Unless we say otherwise, only one code can be used per order, and codes can't be swapped for cash.
  • We may change or end store credit or promotions at any time, but credit already in your account stays usable unless we give you reasonable notice.

Pickup and hand delivery

We'll message you through your order page when your print is ready, and arrange a time to collect it or have it dropped off. If a finished order isn't collected within 60 days of us letting you know it's ready, and we can't reach you after reasonable attempts, we may recycle it.

About 3D printed parts

3D printing builds parts in layers, so prints normally show fine layer lines, small marks where supports were removed, and slight variations in colour, size and finish from the preview or photos. These are normal, not faults. If exact sizes or finishes matter, tell us when you order.

Unless we've agreed otherwise in writing, prints aren't made or tested for:

  • safety-critical or load-bearing uses
  • contact with food or drink
  • high heat, outdoor or UV exposure
  • children under 3 (small parts can be a choking hazard)

If something's wrong with your print, message us from your order page or email us. We'll work out the right fix with you: a reprint, repair or refund.
