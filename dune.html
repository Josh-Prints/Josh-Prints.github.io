How to play
  • Your ball rolls over the dunes on its own.
  • Hold down (touch, click or Space) to get heavy and dive into the hills. Let go to fly off the tops.
  • Land smoothly on a downhill slope to get a speed boost. Land three in a row for a fever combo.
  • Grab the coins on the ground. When you're flying fast, more sometimes appear right on your flight path ahead of you. Land hard (Ouch!) and you'll drop some.
  • Your coins are saved to your account. Spend them in the shop on ball skins and world themes.
  • A sandstorm is following you. Keep your speed up or it catches you.
