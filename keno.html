How to play

  • Pick 1 to 10 numbers from 1 to 80 (or Quick pick), set your bet, then draw.
  • 20 numbers are drawn. The more of yours that come up, the more you win; the pay table shows what each number of hits pays for the spots you picked.
  • Your numbers stay picked, so you can draw again with the same card.
  • Like real keno, it pays back around 90 to 92% for 2 to 10 spots, and 75% for a single spot.
