How to play

  • Bet the Ante, Pair Plus, or both, then deal. You and the dealer each get three cards.
  • After looking at your cards, Play (bet the same as your Ante again) or Fold (lose the Ante).
  • The dealer needs Queen high or better to play. If not, your Ante pays 1 to 1 and the Play bet comes back.
  • If the dealer qualifies, the better hand wins: Ante and Play pay 1 to 1. Ties push.
  • Hands, best first: straight flush, three of a kind, straight, flush, pair, high card. A-2-3 is the lowest straight.
  • Pair Plus pays on your hand alone, whatever the dealer has. The Ante bonus pays even if the dealer wins.
  • Good strategy: play Queen-6-4 or better, fold anything worse.
