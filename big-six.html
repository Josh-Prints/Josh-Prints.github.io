How to play

  • Bet on the symbol you think the wheel will stop on, then spin. The leather pointer at the top picks the winner.
  • Each symbol pays what it says: 1 pays 1 to 1, 2 pays 2 to 1, and so on up to 20. The Joker and the Vox logo pay 40 to 1.
  • The wheel has 54 slots: 24 ones, 15 twos, 7 fives, 4 tens, 2 twenties, 1 Joker and 1 logo, the same as a Vegas Big Six.
  • It's one of the worst bets in the casino: the 1 is the best bet and still loses 11% over time.
