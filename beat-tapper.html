Tips for tight timing

  • Wired headphones or speakers are best. Bluetooth adds delay that browsers can't always measure; if your taps sound late on playback, use the timing nudge.
  • Use "Play back with clicks" after recording. You'll hear a click on every tap. If they sit slightly off the music, change the nudge and play it back again, no need to re-record.
  • Hard to keep up? Choose 75% or 50% speed. The times are saved against the original song, so they still match.
