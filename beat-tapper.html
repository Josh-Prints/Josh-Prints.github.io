4 Your taps

Tips for tight timing

  • Wired headphones or speakers are best. Bluetooth adds delay that browsers can't always measure; if your taps sound late on playback, use the timing nudge.
  • Use "Play my taps" after recording. You'll hear a click on every tap. If they sit slightly off the music, change the nudge and play it back again, no need to re-record.
  • Hard to keep up? Drag the speed slider down (it can go to 25%). The times are saved against the original song, so they still match. Slower playback also lowers the pitch.
  • To layer buttons, record one pass, then press "Record another pass". Your earlier taps play as clicks so you can tap along with them. Untick Rec on buttons you don't want to change.

Calibration

Everyone presses a little early or late, and speakers, Bluetooth and phones add delay. Calibrate once: a click plays, you tap on each click, and the offset is set for you. It is applied to every tap you record.

Negative moves your taps earlier. Positive moves them later.

Takes about 15 seconds. Use the same headphones or speakers you'll record with.
