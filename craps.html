How to play

  • Pass Line (before a point): 7 or 11 wins, 2, 3 or 12 loses. Any other number becomes the point; then the point wins and 7 loses. Pays 1 to 1.
  • Don't Pass is the opposite: 2 or 3 wins, 12 pushes, 7 or 11 loses; after a point, 7 wins.
  • Odds sit behind your line bet once a point is set and pay true odds with no house edge: up to 3× on 4 and 10, 4× on 5 and 9, 5× on 6 and 8. Lay odds on Don't Pass go up to 6×.
  • Come works like the Pass Line, made after the point is set; it moves to its own number.
  • Place a number (tap its box): it wins whenever that number rolls before a 7. 4 and 10 pay 9 to 5, 5 and 9 pay 7 to 5, 6 and 8 pay 7 to 6. Place bets and hardways are off on the come-out roll and stay up after a win. Take down removes them.
  • Field is one roll: 3, 4, 9, 10, 11 pay even money, 2 pays double, 12 triple.
  • Hardways win when the number rolls as a pair (3 + 3 for hard 6) before it rolls the easy way or a 7.
  • One-roll bets win or lose on the next roll. The puck shows the point: OFF before a point, ON over the point number.
